# Retail-Store-Sales-Dashboard
The Retail Store Sales Dashboard is an interactive and visually compelling Power BI dashboard designed to deliver actionable insights for retail businesses. This dashboard focuses on breaking down sales data into meaningful metrics, helping stakeholders understand customer preferences, shipping efficiency, and product profitability at a glance.

Key Features:

Interactive Region Filters:
Separate tabs for Central, East, South, and West regions to compare performance across areas.

Payment Mode Analysis:
Doughnut chart visualizing customer preferences for Cards, Online, and Cash on Delivery (COD) payments.

Monthly Trends:
Line charts for Sales and Profit by month, highlighting seasonal trends and growth opportunities.

Segment and Category Insights:
Pie charts and bar charts showing Sales by Segment (Consumer, Corporate, Home Office) and Category (Office Supplies, Technology, Furniture).

Shipping Performance:
Bar chart detailing the popularity and efficiency of shipping modes like Standard Class and Same Day.

Profitability Heatmap:
Geographical map showcasing state-wise profit and sales, providing clear regional performance indicators.

Sub-Category Spotlight:
Focus on high-performing sub-categories like Phones, Chairs, and Binders for detailed business insights.

How It Helps Businesses:

Identifies sales peaks and lows across months and regions.
Streamlines decision-making on shipping modes and payment preferences.
Highlights high-profit categories and underperforming areas for targeted interventions.
